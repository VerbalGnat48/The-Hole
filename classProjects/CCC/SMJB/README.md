////////// Super Matt-Joe Brothers! //////////

This is me and my partners final project from our spring 2024 semester in Vircon32's assembley language.

It is the first level of the original Super Mario Bros but with a twist. Our two Computer Science Teachers Matt Haas and Joe Openheim from CCC have become been merged with Mario and Luigi to create Mattio and Joeigi respectively. And a final boss battle has been added to the end of the level.

Was made in Spring, 2024 from 04/01 -> 05/01 for our school expo.
